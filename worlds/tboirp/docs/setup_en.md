# The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ Randomizer Setup Guide

## Required Software

- The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth from: [The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth Steam Store Page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/250900/The_Binding_of_Isaac_Rebirth/)
- All DLC is required
- Archipelago Mod for Repentance+
  from: [Repentance+ Archipelago Mod Releases Page](https://github.com/iWasntHere/RepentancePlus-AP-Mod/releases)

## Installation Procedure

1. Unzip the mod into the `mods` directory of your Isaac installation. Ensure that `mods/archipelago` is a valid path.
2. Receive the supplementary mod from the host. It should have a filename similar to `_AP-TBOIRP-###########-PLAYERNAME.zip`.
Similarly to the main Archipelago mod, unzip it into the `mods` directory.
3. Launch the game and enable the mods.
4. In your Archipelago directory, ensure that your `host.yaml`'s `game_directory` under `tboirp_options` is set to your
proper install directory.

## Connecting

1. Launch the TBOI client from the Archipelago launcher. Use the launcher to connect to the host.
2. Begin a new run in Isaac when prompted. You should see in the client that you have connected.

## Resuming

Follow the steps for connecting. The client should prevent you from connecting if you connect to the wrong multiworld.

## Helpful Features

Hold the backslash key (\ or |) to bring up an in-game item tracker page. While holding this key, use
ENTER and RIGHT SHIFT to scroll the pages up and down, and use \[ and \] to
scroll left and right. This helps you know which items are available to your game.
